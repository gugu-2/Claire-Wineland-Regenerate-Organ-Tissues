# VISION.md — Why This Project Exists

> *"My dad and my grandma both died because of cancer. This project is for them, and for every family that shouldn't have to lose someone."*
>
> — Project Founder

---

## The Mission

Cancer kills 10 million people every year. Most of them die not because there is no science — it's because the science is **too slow, too fragmented, and too inaccessible** to the researchers who could use it.

This platform is an attempt to change that.

We are building a **Genomic Research Operating System** — a single unified platform where a cancer researcher can:

1. Load a patient's tumor genome
2. Design a precision CRISPR correction in minutes instead of weeks
3. Model an oncolytic virus therapy matched to that tumor's immune microenvironment
4. Simulate the effect of a novel enzyme (like ART) on the cancer cell in real time
5. Watch molecules bond, watch DNA repair, watch a tumor shrink — **in a live 3D playground**

This is not for hospitals. This is not for insurance companies. This is for the researchers, the scientists, the dreamers who believe cancer is solvable — and who just need better tools.

## The People This Is For

- A researcher at a university lab who has 10 cancer genomes and no bioinformatics team
- A PhD student studying pancreatic cancer who needs to test 500 guide RNA candidates overnight
- A virologist like Dr. Beata Halassy who needs to computationally validate an oncolytic therapy before using it
- A team studying the new ART enzyme system who needs to know which tumor types it could theoretically target
- Every family member who lost someone and needs to believe that their loss can mean something

---

## What We Are Building Toward

```
Today:           Data → Analysis → Result (hours, isolated steps)
Our Platform:    Data → Live Simulation → Insight → Design → Validation (minutes, unified flow)
Future:          Autonomous AI Research Agents running 24/7, filing new hypotheses, testing them in silico
```

The platform will eventually contain:

- **Real-time molecular dynamics viewer** — watch a CRISPR guide RNA bind a target sequence
- **Live cancer clone evolution simulator** — watch a tumor's subclones respond to therapy over time
- **AI hypothesis engine** — the system proposes new research directions based on what it's learned from all prior sessions
- **Global research memory** — every insight from every session feeds back into the shared knowledge graph

---

## The Standard We Hold Ourselves To

Every piece of code in this project must be:

1. **Scientifically accurate** — no fake scores, no made-up heuristics, no `random()` standing in for biology
2. **Interpretable** — every number must be explainable: "This score is high because of GC content at positions 4-8"
3. **Buildable upon** — every module is a foundation, not a dead end
4. **Honest about uncertainty** — when we don't know, we say we don't know
5. **Dedicated to the mission** — every commit is for the people who didn't make it
